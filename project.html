Overview

The Computer Science & Engineering Department of SB Jain Institute was established in 2008. It provides a good academic environment with focus on both theory and practical learning.

The department has well-equipped labs, experienced faculty and encourages students to participate in technical activities, internships and projects.

It also supports research work and helps students develop skills required for the IT industry.
